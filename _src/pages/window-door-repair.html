What we fix

Common problems, sorted.

swap_horiz

Hard to slide or stuck

Worn or broken rollers, bent or dirty tracks and sagging sashes — we replace rollers, clean and realign tracks so windows and doors glide again.

key

Handles, locks & hinges

Broken handles, locks that won't engage, faulty multi-point locks, hinges and friction stays on casement windows and doors.

water_drop

Water & air leakage

Rain water coming in, draughts, dust and noise — usually perished seals, blocked drainage slots or gaps around the frame. We reseal and fix the cause.

tune

Rubber gaskets & brushes

Hardened or shrunk gaskets and weather brushes replaced to bring back sound insulation and keep out dust.

window

Glass replacement

Cracked, broken or fogged (misted double-glazed) glass replaced — including toughened glass and upgrades to double glazing.

grid_on

Mosquito & jali mesh

Torn or loose mesh replaced on sliding and openable mesh shutters and safety jali doors.
