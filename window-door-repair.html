What we fix

Common problems, sorted.

swap_horiz

Hard to slide or stuck

Worn or broken rollers, bent or dirty tracks and sagging sashes — we replace rollers, clean and realign tracks so windows and doors glide again.

key

Handles, locks & hinges

Broken handles, locks that won't engage, faulty multi-point locks, hinges and friction stays on casement windows and doors.

water_drop

Water & air leakage

Rain water coming in, draughts, dust and noise — usually perished seals, blocked drainage slots or gaps around the frame. We reseal and fix the cause.

tune

Rubber gaskets & brushes

Hardened or shrunk gaskets and weather brushes replaced to bring back sound insulation and keep out dust.

window

Glass replacement

Cracked, broken or fogged (misted double-glazed) glass replaced — including toughened glass and upgrades to double glazing.

grid_on

Mosquito & jali mesh

Torn or loose mesh replaced on sliding and openable mesh shutters and safety jali doors.

Questions about repairs

Do you install UPVC windows and doors in Noida?

add

Yes. UPVC Club is based in Greater Noida (Sector Chi V) and installs UPVC windows, doors, balcony glazing and glass partitions across Noida, Greater Noida West (Noida Extension), Greater Noida, Ghaziabad and Delhi, and across the rest of Delhi NCR on request.

How much do UPVC windows cost in Noida?

add

The price depends on the size of each opening, the window style (sliding, casement, tilt & turn), the profile, the glass (single, double glazed, toughened) and the hardware. We do a free site visit to measure every opening and then share an itemised quotation, so you pay only for what your home needs.

Do you repair old UPVC and aluminium windows and doors?

add

Yes. We repair and service UPVC and aluminium windows and doors in Noida, Greater Noida and across Delhi NCR — including ones we didn't install. Common fixes: windows that are hard to slide or won't lock, broken handles, locks and hinges, worn rollers and tracks, water or air leakage, replacing rubber gaskets and seals, cracked or fogged glass, and torn mosquito mesh. Book a repair visit and our technician will inspect and quote before starting work.

Are UPVC windows good for Delhi NCR's heat, dust and noise?

add

Yes. UPVC is a natural insulator, and our multi-chamber profiles with sealed double glazing keep heat and dust out, reduce outside traffic noise and lower air-conditioning load. UPVC does not rust, rot or need painting, and it is termite-proof.

Can I see how a window will look in my home before ordering?

add

Yes. Use the free Try at Home tool on our website: open your phone camera (or upload a photo of your room), drag the frame onto your window or wall, and switch between styles, frame colours and glass types. You can save the photo and send it to us for a quote.

Do you do balcony covering in Noida high-rise societies?

add

Yes. We cover balconies with slim UPVC or aluminium sliding glass systems that keep out dust, rain, pigeons and noise while keeping the view. Our team measures on site and advises on what your society allows.

How long does installation take, and can I track my order?

add

It depends on the number and size of windows. Your quotation includes the timeline, and you can follow every stage — approval, measurement, fabrication, ready, installation — on the Track Order page with your order number.
